# Fieldwork Report: NIT Jamshedpur Night Utility Canteen

## 1. Setting and Context
* **Location:** Night Canteen Counter situated behind Hostel C , D &M  National Institute of Technology Jamshedpur, Adityapur, Jharkhand.
* **Date & Observation Window:** Fieldwork conducted during the evening kitchen setup and dinner preparation transition (6:40 PM – 7:15 PM).
* **Documentary Evidence:**
  * `assets/nitjsr_rate_notice.jpg`: Official Institute standardized food rate circular (Notice Ref: NITJSR/PDD/253/2026, issued by Dean, Planning and Development).
  * `assets/mess_menu_board.jpg`: Photograph of the laminated September 2026 Mess Menu board for Hostels A, B, C, D, M, RLB, and AHR.

## 2. In-Person Conversation
* **Person:** Manoj Kumar
* **Role:** Night Utility Canteen Vendor & Operator (Hostel C/D block)

### Three Specific Statements from Manoj Bhaiya:
1. *"Saturday ko bache Khichdi bilkul nahi khate. Main har Saturday 11 baje tak thak ke dukan band karne ki sochta tha, par achanak 11:30 baje Fried Rice aur Chowmein ki itni bheed aati thi ki boiled rice aur ande khatam ho jate the."*
2. *"Wednesday ko Paneer ya Chicken banta hai mess mein. Us din chahe main kitni bhi achi dukan saja loon, bacha mess se pet bhar ke nikalta hai aur yahan bas 10 rupaye ki chai peene aata hai."*
3. *"Official rate circular ke mutabiq Egg Fried Rice full plate ₹90 aur Egg Chowmein full plate ₹90 ka hai. Khichdi wale din agar meri tayari achi ho to 25-30 plate extra bik jati hain, jisse ₹2,500 se zyada ka sidha munafa hota hai jo pehle stockout ki wajah se chhut jata tha."*

### One Insight That Surprised Me:
I initially hypothesized that Sunday nights would produce the largest revenue surge of the week due to the weekend leisure mood. 
Manoj Bhaiya corrected this immediately: **Sunday is actually one of his slowest nights (averaging ~₹2,000)** because the hostel mess serves a popular dinner of Puri, Chana, and Suji Halwa with dry fruits, and students who skip mess either go to Bistupur market or pool money for Swiggy/Zomato deliveries. His real goldmine shifts are **Saturday (Khichdi night)** and **Tuesday (Lauki Chana night)**.

## 3. Raw Data Collection (`data.csv`) Methodology
The raw data in `data.csv` was compiled directly at the canteen counter:
1. Quantities for the top fast-food movers (Veg Fried Rice, Egg Fried Rice, Veg Chowmein, Egg Chowmein, Omelettes, Veg Maggi) were extracted from Manoj Bhaiya's physical bill receipts, counter order chits, and UPI transaction logs across 21 consecutive operating days (September 1 to September 21, 2026).
2. Prices and revenue figures strictly adhere to the standardized rate sheet finalized by the NIT Jamshedpur Students' Council and approved by the Dean (P&D).
3. Mess dishes were cross-referenced with the official hostel mess menu schedule.
